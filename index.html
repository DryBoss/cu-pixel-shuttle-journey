CU SHUTTLE — DRIVER'S MANUAL

Chittagong University shuttle · Bottoli to CU · 9 stops

1. YOUR JOB

Take the students from Bottoli to the university. Stop at every platform, wait while they climb aboard, leave on the guard's flag, keep to the timetable and the speed limits, and bring everyone in smoothly. Nobody gets off on the way: at CU everyone gets off.

3. GETTING AWAY

  1. Start the engine and wait for the lamp to turn green.
  2. Release the brake (REL). The air takes a moment to bleed off.
  3. Wait for the guard's flag (the FLAG lamp). Leaving early costs points, and so does leaving anyone waiting on the platform.
  4. Open the throttle gently. The wheels slip if you give too much power at low speed: above notch 7 on dry rails, above notch 5 on wet rails (Monsoon). If the SLIP lamp lights, drop to notch 3 (2 when wet) until they grip.

4. ON THE LINE

  • The train is heavy. It is slow to pick up speed and it coasts for minutes, so plan well ahead.
  • Speed limits: 100 on the open line, 80 in the hills, 60 over bridges and near stations, 50 on the climb into CU. Going over the limit costs points. The SPEED lamp flashes.
  • Air brakes lag. Nothing happens for about a second after you apply them, they build up slowly, and they release slowly. Start braking early and in steps, roughly half a kilometre before a platform when running at full speed.
  • Coupler slack: never slam from heavy power straight to heavy brake (or back). Pass through notch 0 and pause for about a second.
  • The grade: the last climb to CU drags the train back (GRADE lamp). Keep power on and hold your speed.
  • Jerky speed changes, wheel slip and slack bangs all use up the comfort meter.

5. STOPPING AT A PLATFORM

Bring the train to a halt with the cab alongside the platform mark. Stopping anywhere inside the platform window counts, and the nearer the mark the better. Once you stand still the students board. Hold the brake until the FLAG lamp lights, then go. At CU, stop before the buffers: hitting them is a big penalty.

7. THE AUTOPILOT

Press C (or the DRIVE button) to hand over. It drives with the same physics: it respects the limits, brakes in good time, stops at each platform, and works the headlight for you. Take back the controls any time.

Press K or Esc to close · the train is paused while you read.
